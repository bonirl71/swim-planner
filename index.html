📖 Guide

How to use the Swim Planner

🌊 Check current conditions

Choose a swim location and select Check Swim Conditions. The app will show the latest available weather, wind, water and sea conditions.

📅 Plan a future sea swim

Sea locations show high and low tides for the next 7 days. Select the high tide you want to swim around.

🔮 Predict future conditions

Select a future high tide and choose Predict Swim Conditions. The app will show the expected conditions around that time.

⚠️ Forecast guidance only

Forecast conditions, particularly several days in advance, can change significantly. Predictions are a guide only and should not be relied upon as an assessment of swimming safety. Always check the latest forecast and local conditions before swimming.

🚗 Plan your journey

Select your chosen high tide and use Calculate Journey From My Location to calculate your driving distance, journey time and recommended departure time.

The journey planner currently works on the basis of arriving 2 hours before high tide.

🏞️ Lake swimming

Lake locations don't use tidal information. Instead, the app assesses wind, expected chop, temperature and weather conditions.

It can also indicate which side of the lake may be more sheltered depending on the wind direction.

🔄 Changing location

When you change location, any previous Journey Planner and future prediction are automatically cleared.

🏊 Worked Example — Planning a Sea Swim

Here's an example of how you can use the app to plan a future swim at Whitestrand.

1️⃣ Select Whitestrand

From Choose Swim Location, select Whitestrand (Clare).

2️⃣ Check the conditions

Tap Check Swim Conditions. The app will show the current weather, wind, waves, water temperature and upcoming tides.

3️⃣ Choose your future high tide

In the Journey Planner, look at the high tides available for the next 7 days. Select the future date and time you want to swim.

For example, you might choose a Saturday morning high tide if you're planning your weekend swim.

4️⃣ Predict the swimming conditions

With your future high tide selected, tap 🔮 Predict Swim Conditions.

The app will estimate what the conditions could be like at that time, including wind, waves, water temperature, rain and the predicted Swim Score.

This gives you an early indication of whether the swim looks promising.

5️⃣ Plan your journey

If the predicted conditions look suitable, tap 📍 Calculate Journey From My Location.

The app will calculate the driving distance and estimated journey time, then work backwards from the selected high tide to tell you when you should leave home.

The planner allows 2 hours before high tide for your arrival at the swim location.

6️⃣ Check again before you swim

A forecast several days ahead is only a guide. Check the latest conditions again on the day of your swim before deciding whether to enter the water.
