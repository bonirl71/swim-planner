📖 How to use

Start by choosing your swim location.

🌊 Sea swims

Check the current weather, wind, waves, water temperature and tides. Select a future high tide to predict conditions, then use the Journey Planner if you are travelling to the swim.

🏞️ Lake swimming

Lake locations use weather, wind and the lake's existing shelter guidance. The Lake Forecast shows the expected conditions for the next 7 days.

🔮 Forecasts

Forecasts are guidance only and can change, especially several days ahead. Always check the latest forecast and local conditions before swimming.

🔄 Changing location

Changing location clears the previous forecast, prediction and journey information.
